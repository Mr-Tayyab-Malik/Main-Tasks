2. Walnut

Walnuts are unique among dry fruits because of their brain-like shape and their rich content of omega-3 fatty acids, which are important for brain and heart health. They have a slightly earthy and rich flavor that makes them stand out from other nuts. Walnuts contain antioxidants, protein, fiber, vitamins, and minerals that contribute to overall well-being. Regular consumption of walnuts may help support memory, concentration, and cardiovascular health. They can be enjoyed on their own as a snack or added to salads, oatmeal, desserts, and baked goods.
