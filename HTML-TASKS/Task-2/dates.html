3. Dates

Dates are naturally sweet fruits that have been enjoyed for thousands of years and remain an important part of many cultures around the world. They are often called nature's candy because of their rich sweetness, yet they also provide valuable nutrients such as fiber, potassium, magnesium, antioxidants, and natural sugars that offer quick energy. Dates are especially popular during fasting periods because they help restore energy levels rapidly while being gentle on the stomach. Their soft texture and caramel-like taste make them a delicious snack on their own, and they are also widely used in desserts, smoothies, and healthy recipes.
