4. Pistachio

Pistachios are among the most enjoyable dry fruits because of their distinctive green color, naturally split shells, and delicious nutty flavor. They are rich in protein, fiber, healthy fats, vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants that help support overall health. Pistachios can help satisfy hunger while providing important nutrients, making them a popular choice for healthy snacking. Their combination of taste and nutrition has made them a favorite in many cuisines around the world, where they are used in desserts, ice creams, salads, and savory dishes.
